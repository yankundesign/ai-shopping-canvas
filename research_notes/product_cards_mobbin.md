# Product cards on Mobbin

Status: research, 2026-11-26. How products are shown with a price and details, read against our card in [MVP_SCOPE.md](../MVP_SCOPE.md): photo, name, price, store, link and details. Not on the moodboard yet. Links open on Mobbin.

## The photo

- **Klarna.** Every product sits on the same soft grey rounded plate, as a cutout. Sneakers, earbuds and phones from different sellers look like one set. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/35cf8624-dbaf-4865-a0b6-1a2a3fabde74)
- **Shop (iOS).** The same idea, warmer: a rounded plate and a heart in the corner. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/7991e573-6167-4111-9115-68cafe6c5c50)
- **ZARA, H&M, Urban Outfitters.** Full-bleed model shots, with almost no text below them. This works only because one studio shot every photo. [ZARA](https://mobbin.com/screens/136e0aee-422a-4fa9-a1c9-7fadb5ce5541) · [H&M](https://mobbin.com/screens/78324c01-0bb7-412e-8976-acf218a96265) · [UO](https://mobbin.com/screens/b427285b-3a7a-4224-b33c-b7dd0c910eb8)

Our photos come from three sources (store page, Shopify catalog, the agent), so we are Klarna's case, not ZARA's.

## The price

- **Strikethroughs everywhere:** Etsy, H&M, Amazon, Walmart, Selfridges. This is sale theatre. [Amazon](https://mobbin.com/screens/313268ae-15f3-4e13-a31e-fcc7e11211e3)
- **Unit price.** Amazon's Rufus shows "$44.64 ($0.62/ounce)". Blinkit shows "58 g · ₹21 · Inclusive of all taxes". When pack sizes differ, this is the number that compares. [Rufus](https://mobbin.com/screens/ace833cd-b503-42e6-a317-16c4a2d3a126) · [Blinkit](https://mobbin.com/screens/34d2aa72-084a-4a35-8ac7-aa3dfabc3e80)
- **One product, many stores.** KAYAK shows one big price with a small row beneath ("Expedia $63 · KAYAK $63 · Budget $65"). Klarna lists each seller as a row with shipping and price. [KAYAK](https://mobbin.com/screens/b80d8dff-2698-42b3-9bac-891c7227a14f) · [Klarna](https://mobbin.com/screens/4786a4a1-77e7-42a0-a794-792120c9c20f)
- **Trust, as a disclaimer.** Tabby's details sheet ends with "Displayed prices may be outdated. Check the partner's online store for current prices." That is honest, but it applies to every price at once. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/9a01c90c-7878-429d-adcf-7ddeeb54839e)
- **Price as a job for the agent.** Copilot's sheet sets a price goal ("20% off ($118.40)") and runs for six months. It belongs to the delegated tempo, and it's later work. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/7cc690bf-ad17-4c3d-9a3e-b159dc331372)

## Details

- **ChatGPT shopping research.** One product card with label and value rows (Flavour, Type, Brand, Size) beside the photo. This is the closest to our details as lines. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/740b0b9f-805d-46c6-86c1-de07d8b5907b)
- **Etsy collection.** Even a grid card carries two label lines: "Shipping: Digital Download", "Returns: Not accepted". [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/0fb8fb62-b817-4ace-a62e-dcedb502ccd4)
- **Klarna.** Specs as one grey comma line ("Wireless, In-Ear, Microphone…"). Compact, but nothing lines up from card to card.
- **Walmart "Compare with similar items".** Products across the top, attribute rows below, and "-" where a value is missing. "-" can mean not found or not applicable, and you can't tell which. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/1659469a-cccf-407e-8c11-b227303fad1e)
- **Thrive Market.** Yes/no facts as icons with a word: Gluten-Free, Dairy-Free, Vegetarian. [Screen](https://mobbin.com/screens/34da6d69-de17-4991-a11b-93a5a3e59139)

## Tags and actions

- **One tag, top-left of the photo:** "Bestseller" (Faire, Walmart), "Popular" (Klarna), "Great Deal" (KAYAK). No card carries two. [Walmart](https://mobbin.com/screens/92dd3e36-3e2d-42e4-8e80-bce39fb70c48)
- **Actions:** nearly every retail card has a cart button, "Options" or "+" (Walmart, CVS, Amazon iOS, Shipt). Urban Outfitters shows "Quick Shop" on hover. Selfridges' wishlist card has an × to remove it and "View Details". [CVS](https://mobbin.com/screens/6a169098-caaf-4690-aa6f-63ca077b8563) · [Selfridges wishlist](https://mobbin.com/screens/30b6370b-27ab-4cb8-93c6-710d372a053b)

## What I'd take

1. **One plate for every photo** (Klarna). A neutral square with the photo contained, so three photo sources look like one set. When the photo is credited, the credit goes under the plate, not on it.
2. **Name first, then one quiet line: "$148 · REI"** (ChatGPT). On our canvas, the first question is "is this the right product?", and only then "how much?". Keep the store, because the same product may come from two stores.
3. **Put where the price came from on the price line**, not in a disclaimer like Tabby's: "$148 · REI · read 2h ago" or "$148 · from Claude, not checked". No strikethroughs, because we don't sell.
4. **Details as label and value lines, in the same order on every card**, so the eye can run across the canvas like Walmart's rows. The value slot does the work: waiting, the answer with its source, or "Couldn't find". Never Walmart's "-".
5. **One tag slot, top-left of the photo**, for the canvas's own "Cheapest" or "Lightest" in the finalists. Same place, same count, as Bestseller.
6. **No cart button and no heart.** Clicking a card selects it, and the store link is the card's only button. Every retail card here has a cart button, and on our card it would compete with pointing.
7. **Later: unit price on the compare line.** When pack sizes differ, the canvas can place cards by "$ per ounce", not the price alone (Rufus, Blinkit).
